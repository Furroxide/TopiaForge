## Keyboard, assistive technology and visual oracles

1. **Navigation and focus:** use Tab/Shift+Tab, Ctrl+1…8 for the currently visible section order, Alt+Left/Right cycling and each control's normal activation keys. Verify Developer-mode changes update that visible order and restore sensible focus. Focus must be visible, follow a usable order, enter dialogs, remain within an active modal, and return to the trigger or a sensible surviving target on cancel/complete. Test route changes, list refresh/removal, busy completion, error recovery, window deactivation/reactivation and Developer-mode removal. No keyboard trap, hidden focus or inaccessible required action passes.
2. **Screen reader:** confirm the window/route, names, roles, values and selected/checked/expanded/disabled states are exposed. Read inputs and associated validation/help, table/list context, full truncated content, modal title/body/buttons, progress, error/recovery and result feedback. Check icon-only buttons and links. Record missing, stale, duplicated or misleading announcements and exact speech/transcript observations using synthetic data; never infer coverage from Flutter semantics alone. If Unity surfaces are not exposed, record that as an accessibility gap rather than assuming game UI is exempt.
3. **Text and layout:** inspect actual rendered labels, long/unbroken paths and package names, multiline messages, scrolling and visible focus at every required configuration. Access full content without hiding primary actions or forcing impossible scrolling. Record measured viewport/text settings and unmasked originals. A visual baseline needs reviewer acceptance; new screenshots do not approve themselves.
4. **Contrast:** propose WCAG 2.2 AA contrast measurements as a review rubric: at least 4.5:1 for ordinary text, 3:1 for large text (18 pt regular or 14 pt bold), and 3:1 for essential non-text control/state information against adjacent colors, applying the stated exceptions. Measure effective rendered colors/backgrounds, including overlay compositing, normal/high-contrast themes and focused/selected states; do not round a failing ratio upward. These checks adapt the [W3C text contrast](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/contrast-minimum.html) and [non-text contrast](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/non-text-contrast.html) criteria for this native QA brief; they do not establish whole-product WCAG conformance. The [W3C resize-text criterion](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/resize-text.html) also supports the 200% without loss-of-function check already required by the repository.
5. **Motion and status:** verify reduced motion removes nonessential movement without removing state information; motion intensity changes remaining supported effects. Success, warning, error, disabled and destructive states retain clear words/icons and accessible feedback. Long operations expose understandable progress and completion/failure without repeated disruptive announcements.
