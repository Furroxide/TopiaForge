# Trademarks and affiliation

This is the canonical wording. Every surface that shows a non-affiliation notice — the README, the
desktop launcher, the in-game manager, the support page, the release archive — states one of the two
forms below, unchanged. Editing this file is how that wording changes everywhere.

## Full statement

Shown wherever there is room for it, and behind the link from the short form.

> TopiaForge is an independent, community-built modding toolkit. It is not developed, published, or
> endorsed by Tomato Cake or the Robotopia development team, and is not otherwise affiliated with
> them. "Robotopia" and "Tomato Cake" are the property of their respective owners and are used here
> only to identify the game this toolkit works with.

## Short statement

For a prominent but space-constrained surface: the in-game manager, a launcher footer, a settings
row. It is only complete when the full statement is one click away, so it always appears with a
link or button labelled **Trademarks and affiliation** pointing at this page.

> TopiaForge is an independent project, not affiliated with or endorsed by Tomato Cake.

The short form drops the ownership sentence and the nominative-use rationale. That is a deliberate
trade, and it is what the adjacent link exists to repair — a surface that shows the short form
without the link is not showing the notice.

## Support routing

Add this wherever a reader could plausibly go looking for help, so a player who mistakes TopiaForge
for an official product does not file it against the wrong people. It is contextual, not part of
the statement itself.

> Please do not contact Tomato Cake or the Robotopia development team about TopiaForge.
> Support for this toolkit is described in [SUPPORT.md](SUPPORT.md).

## Bundled brand assets

The statement above covers names and affiliation. It does **not** cover the artwork this project
bundles. Three Robotopia web images ship with the launcher under attribution while a written grant
remains outstanding; they are recorded, with their rights basis and dated disposition, in
[THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md](THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md).

## Status

The wording here has not been reviewed by counsel. `P0-IP-01` in

and the rights basis for compatibility work, and it remains open and blocking. Keeping the text in
