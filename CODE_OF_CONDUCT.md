# Community Code of Conduct

TopiaForge's community should be welcoming, technically honest, and safe for people with different backgrounds and
experience levels.

Participants must communicate respectfully, critique work rather than people, respect privacy and attribution, avoid
harassment or discriminatory conduct, and stop disruptive behavior when asked. Deliberate security abuse, publishing
private information, plagiarism, license violations, and attempts to pressure maintainers into unsafe releases are not
acceptable.

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, and project-run community spaces.
Maintainers may edit or remove content, pause interactions, reject contributions, or restrict participation when needed
to protect the community and project.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through GitHub. Do not place sensitive personal details in a
public issue. The owner should document a dedicated confidential conduct contact before opening a larger public
community.
